A friendly introduction

Creativity with a little extra fluff.

The furry fandom is a community of people who enjoy fictional animal characters with human traits. That interest can mean drawing, writing, making costumes, role-playing, meeting friends—or simply enjoying the art.

Many ways to join in

What kinds of furries are there?

There aren’t official “types” or a single way to be a furry. People often describe themselves by the activities they enjoy or the characters they create.

Fursuiters

Fans who wear a custom animal-character costume called a fursuit. Some wear a full suit; others choose a head, paws, ears, or tail.

Artists & makers

People who draw characters, write stories, craft costumes, sculpt, sew, animate, or build other creative projects.

Character creators

Many fans invent a personal character, often called a fursona. It might be a wolf, fox, deer, dragon, or something entirely original.

A community, not a costume

What brings people together?

Shared creativity and enthusiasm for anthropomorphic characters. Furry fans connect online and at meetups or conventions, where activities can include art shows, games, costume gatherings, and charity events.

Some people are deeply involved; others just enjoy a few artworks or characters. Both are perfectly fine.

How people behave

People are people—be respectful.

There isn’t one personality or behavior shared by all furries. As in any community, people have different interests and comfort levels. Friendly basics go a long way:

Ask before hugging or touching

Fursuits can limit vision and be warm to wear. Always get consent before touching someone or their costume.

Respect names and boundaries

Some fans use a character name or prefer not to be photographed. A quick, polite question is best.

Remember the suit is a costume

A person in a fursuit is still a person. Don’t assume their character tells you everything about them.

Enjoy the creativity

Share art kindly, credit creators, and let people enjoy the fandom in their own way.

Quick myth-buster: being a furry usually means being a fan of anthropomorphic animal characters. It doesn’t mean someone owns a fursuit or behaves like an animal.
